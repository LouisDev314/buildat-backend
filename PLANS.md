## Outstanding decisions and risks

Resolve decisions at their dependent gate, not by silently encoding defaults. The [decision register](docs/DECISIONS.md) owns statuses and rationale. Highest-priority open items are authentication, owner/manager/member authority, PostgreSQL version and migration history, isolated test setup, and API/error contracts. Later items include skills vocabulary, project publication requirements, application/capacity semantics, evidence review, notification channel, retention/moderation and alpha targets.

Material risks: undocumented external database state; context tests requiring local database credentials; dependencies present without product security; premature schema design locking in unresolved membership rules; unreliable external evidence; recruitment with insufficient suitable participants; abandoned collaborations; moderation burden and privacy/history conflicts. Mitigate through explicit decisions, small verified slices and alpha learning. Do not add infrastructure to compensate for unvalidated demand.
