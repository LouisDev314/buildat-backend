# Product

This document owns product intent and positioning. [MVP.md](MVP.md) defines the initial scope; [DOMAIN.md](DOMAIN.md) defines the concepts; [BUSINESS.md](BUSINESS.md) owns commercial hypotheses. This is agreed direction, not a description of shipped functionality.

## Thesis and initial audience

Buildat is a project-first collaboration network. The initial audience is indie-game creators assembling small teams and builders seeking work suited to their skills, interests, availability and desired commitment. Builders can include programmers, artists, designers, writers, audio specialists and producers; these examples do not establish a fixed skill taxonomy.

The primary object people discover is a credible project with a clear scope, current stage, evidence and specific openings. Profiles explain who can contribute, while collaboration history explains what people have actually delivered. AI may help express scope and role requirements or explain compatibility; the product must work without AI.

## Problems and positioning

Creators need to communicate a realistic project, recruit for bounded responsibilities, assess applicants and maintain continuity when team members join or leave. Contributors need to judge whether expectations, available time, compensation disclosures and team practices fit before applying. Both need evidence that collaboration produces work, rather than relying on unsupported claims or popularity.

Buildat connects discovery, recruitment, membership and delivery history in one project context. It is not a full production management suite. The MVP records basic milestones and contributions while teams may continue to use external chat, repositories, issue trackers and asset tools.

## Competitive substitutes

Substitutes include community forums and social groups for recruitment, general professional networks for identity, portfolio sites and code repositories for work samples, freelance marketplaces for paid engagements, and chat/issue tools for coordination. This is a qualitative description of alternatives, not verified competitor research or a claim about market share or any vendor's current features.

The differentiation to validate is continuity: a role leads to an application, an accepted applicant becomes a collaborator, and collaboration yields attributable evidence and project history. Better forms or AI-generated descriptions alone are not a durable advantage. The long-term asset is the project graph linking people, roles, collaborations, dependencies and delivered work, with evidence provenance and permission boundaries intact.

## Journeys and network loop

Creators describe a project and provide evidence, publish explicit roles, review applications, accept collaborators, agree on milestones and acknowledge contributions. They update project history as the team delivers or changes.

Contributors describe their skills and constraints, inspect projects and openings, compare deterministic compatibility signals, apply, join when accepted, and attach evidence to contributions. They carry an attributable history into future collaborations, subject to privacy and moderation rules.

The core loop is: credible project -> relevant opening -> suitable applicant -> accepted collaboration -> meaningful deliverable -> trustworthy history -> better future discovery and recruitment. A registration, page view or long browsing session does not establish that this loop works.

## Long-term vision

Expand beyond indie games only when the initial collaboration loop has evidence of value. Improve discovery using demonstrated work and collaboration outcomes without turning reputation into a popularity contest. Keep account age, membership duration, self-reported hours, acknowledged contributions and independently verified evidence distinct. Owner approval is an acknowledgment, not independent verification.

Funding may later help credible teams deliver whole projects or dependent milestones. It is a separate expansion subject to the prerequisites in [BUSINESS.md](BUSINESS.md); it does not justify money movement, investment infrastructure or fundraising features in the MVP.
